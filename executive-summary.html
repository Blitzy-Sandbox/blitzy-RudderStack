RE-RUN PLAYBOOK

Operational Onboarding

  1. 1 Activate the Semgrep venv source /tmp/semgrep-config-b/.semgrep-venv/bin/activate
  2. 2 Refresh rule packs (optional) curl -sSL https://semgrep.dev/c/p/<slug> -o local-rules/<slug>.yaml
  3. 3 Re-run the scan semgrep scan --config=./local-rules --sarif -o results-semgrep.sarif --metrics=off --exclude=local-rules --exclude=findings-config-b.json .
  4. 4 Normalize findings python3 normalize-sarif.py results-semgrep.sarif findings-config-b.json
