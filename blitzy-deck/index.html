Risk & onboarding

What to watch, what to do next

Risks & mitigations

  • Token absence — SNYK_TOKEN not set; operator must inject.
  • Network egress — Snyk needs snyk.io reachable.
  • Higher findings — .snyk rules expired; expect more than baselines.
  • Exit code 1 is success — snyk test exits 1 on findings; ≥2 is fatal.

Take action

  • Provision SNYK_TOKEN as an environment secret
  • Run the two scans, then python3 scripts/normalize-snyk-findings.py
  • Commit findings-config-h.json and DECISIONS.md
