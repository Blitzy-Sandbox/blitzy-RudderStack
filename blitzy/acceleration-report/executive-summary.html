  • Linear API unavailable → M12 (Defects Out of SLA) reports Insufficient signal — no SLA source. M6 confidence reduced from High to Medium.
  • Admin audit log unavailable → M10 (Approved Exceptions) confidence Low. Required-review bypass detection and protected-branch policy events not visible.
